# Writing a post

Erwann brings the thinking, an AI brings the prose. This file holds the few
things that turned out to matter, so they don't have to be said every time.

Written 2026-09-04, from one post. **Revisit after post three: any section that
hasn't changed a draft in three posts gets cut.**

## What only Erwann can supply

- **The annoyance.** Why the post exists. A manufactured grievance reads as
  manufactured.
- **The incident, with its real details.** An invented illustration is how a
  blog ends up sounding like a competent stranger.
- **The verdict.** What he actually thinks is true, including the unflattering
  part.
- **The gap in the argument.** Not style — reasoning. On the first post it was
  *"we're mostly talking about AI reading it, but also committing"*: a hole only
  the person holding the belief could see, and he saw it by reading a draft.

Everything else is the writer's: structure, transitions, rhythm, secondary
examples.

**The first draft exists to be argued with, not approved.** Draft into the
Notion `Blog` database as `Status: Draft`; Erwann reads, corrects, publishes.

## Before handing over a draft

Both of these come from real failures on the first post:

- **Is the other half of the mechanism covered — including the more dangerous
  half?** The draft explained agents *reading* secrets and omitted agents
  *committing* them, which is worse and likelier.
- **Is the list of examples complete, or does a gap undercut the claim?** The
  deny-rule examples omitted the Keychain, which is precisely where a secret
  lives once it isn't in a file.

## Length

**~2,000–2,500 words.** 1,200 was rejected as "not really enough"; the rewrite
at roughly double worked.

## Tics

Measured, not guessed. Run **`/k-deslop`** before publishing.

- **Contractions.** He writes "don't" and "it's"; the first draft said "do not"
  and "that is" throughout, and read starched.
- **"It's not X, it's Y."** Four times in one draft. Once is a sentence.
- **The aphoristic closer.** The neat final line that sounds profound and says
  nothing.

**A tic joins this list when `/k-deslop` has flagged it twice, across two
different posts.** Not from an instinct at midnight.

## Pointers, not copies

- **British English** — `CLAUDE.md` in this repo, in full.
- **No employer names, internal hostnames, or private URL shapes.** Load-bearing
  here rather than advisory: this blog is public and indexed. Restate any real
  case with a neutral vendor, then check the restatement still demonstrates the
  point.
