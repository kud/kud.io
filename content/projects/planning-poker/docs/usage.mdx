Everything on this page is open to every player. Controls that only the host sees are on [🎩 Host controls](/projects/planning-poker/docs/host-controls).

## 🚪 Joining a room

From the landing page, choose **Create room** or **Join room**, give your name and, to join, the room code.

- Room codes are six characters drawn from `A–Z` and `2–9`, with the easily confused `I`, `L`, `O`, `0` and `1` left out, so they survive being read aloud. Codes are case-insensitive.
- A room's address is `/room/<code>`, so the link works just as well as the code.
- Your name and pixel-art avatar are remembered in the browser and can be changed mid-session from **Your profile**.
- If two people pick the same name, the room makes the second one unique for you.
- Opening the room in a second tab keeps you as one player, not two.

If the host has turned on join approval, you wait at the door until they let you in.

## 🗳️ Voting

Pick a card from **Your hand**. Your card lands face-down at your seat; the rest of the table sees that you have voted, not what. You can change your mind freely until the reveal.

### ⌨️ Hotkeys

| Key        | Who     | What it does                                                        |
| ---------- | ------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| `1`–`9`    | Players | Vote the first to ninth card of the deck                            |
| `0`        | Players | Vote the tenth card                                                 |
| `R`        | Host    | Reveal, once at least one vote is in                                |
| `Space`    | Host    | Reveal; after the reveal, next speaker; once everyone has spoken, new round |
| `N`        | Host    | New round, after the reveal                                         |

Hotkeys are ignored while you are typing in a field, when a modifier key is held, and for spectators.

## 👀 Playing or watching

The **Your role** switch next to the table lets you sit out. A spectator holds no cards, any vote they had is dropped, and they are skipped by the speaker roll. It suits a facilitator who wants to watch without skewing the numbers.

## 🎤 The speaker ritual

After the reveal, the host passes the mic round the table:

1. The highest numeric vote explains first.
2. The lowest numeric vote goes second.
3. Everyone else follows in random order.

Non-numeric cards such as `?`, `☕` or T-shirt sizes are picked at random. Spectators are never called.

## ☕ Breaks

Anyone can **ask the room for a coffee break**. It needs a majority of the room (more than half of everyone present) to accept; a majority declining cancels it. An accepted break runs for five minutes, which the host can shorten or extend. The host can end a break at any time, and whoever asked can cancel their own request.

## 🎉 Reactions and the table

- Send a quick reaction (👍 😂 😮 🤯) or pick one from **More reactions**; it floats up for the whole room.
- When everyone votes the same value, confetti fires.
- The croupier, the cat and the plants can be poked, and everyone sees who did it. Play `☕` and the waiter brings a coffee.
- Sounds can be muted with one click, and the choice is remembered. The light or dark theme is remembered too.
