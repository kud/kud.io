---
title: 👋 Introduction
description: A small CSS design system for Firefox WebExtension popups and options pages.
---

webext-ui is two stylesheets, `tokens.css` and `webext-ui.css`, that give a WebExtension popup or options page a shared, accessible look. It ships a tiny CLI that copies the files into your repo, or plain subpath exports if you use a bundler.

## What's here

- [🔌 Getting started](/projects/webext-ui/docs/usage) — vendoring with the `sync` CLI, importing through a bundler, and linking the files on every page
- [🎨 Theming](/projects/webext-ui/docs/theming) — the design tokens, your extension's accent, and the contrast rules behind them
- [🧱 Components](/projects/webext-ui/docs/components) — the class-based primitives: layout, rows, buttons, forms, switch, badge and status dot
