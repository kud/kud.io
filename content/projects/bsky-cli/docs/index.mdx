description: A Bluesky client for your terminal, with one-shot commands for scripts and a full-screen interface for browsing.
---

**bsky-cli** puts Bluesky in your terminal under a single binary, `bsky`. Give it a subcommand and it does one thing and exits, which suits scripts and quick posts; give it nothing and it opens a full-screen client for reading your timeline, replying and liking.

Both modes sign in the same way, with a Bluesky App Password read from your environment, so set that up first.

## 🧭 What's here

- [🔐 Authentication](/projects/bsky-cli/docs/authentication): the two environment variables `bsky` needs, and what goes wrong without them.
- [⚡ Usage](/projects/bsky-cli/docs/usage): every subcommand, from `post` and `thread` to `timeline`, `search` and `follow`.
- [🖥️ Interactive mode](/projects/bsky-cli/docs/interactive): the full-screen client and its keys.
