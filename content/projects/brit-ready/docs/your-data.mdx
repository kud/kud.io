## 💾 Everything stays on your device

There is no account and no backend. Progress is saved in your browser's IndexedDB under the key `brit-ready-progress`, so it belongs to that browser on that device. Clearing site data, or switching browser or device, starts you from scratch unless you restore a backup.

What is saved:

- your name and target test date, if you gave them,
- your preferences: reminders, sound and mascot style,
- XP, streak and the day you last practised,
- mastery for every fact you have seen,
- every session and every answer,
- the questions you last got wrong, and your unlocked badges.

The app installs as a PWA and its service worker caches what it needs, so after the first visit it works offline.

## 📦 Back up and restore

Open **Settings**, then **Your data**:

- **Export** downloads `brit-ready-backup.json`, a readable JSON file of your progress.
- **Import** reads such a file back in and replaces the matching fields.

An import is accepted only if the file says it comes from `brit-ready` and uses the same backup format version (currently `1`); anything else is rejected and your progress is left alone. Reminders, sound and mascot style are not part of the backup, so set them again on a new device.

To move to a new phone or browser, export on the old one, then import on the new one.

## ⚙️ Settings

| Setting            | What it does                                                                    |
| ------------------ | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Your name          | Used to greet you on the home screen; optional                                  |
| Test date          | Your booked test date; drives the countdown in reminders                        |
| Theme              | Light, dark or system                                                           |
| Language           | English only                                                                    |
| Mascot             | Pixel art or soft style for the coach                                           |
| Sound effects      | Subtle sounds for answers and wins; on by default                               |
| Reminders          | Practice nudges, off by default                                                 |
| Reset all progress | Erases everything stored on this device, after a confirmation; cannot be undone |

## 🔔 How reminders work

A PWA cannot schedule notifications in the background without a server, so Brit Ready shows its reminder **when you open the app**, at most once per calendar day. Turning reminders on asks for notification permission; the switch is disabled in browsers that do not support notifications.

- With a test date set, the reminder counts down the days to your test.
- Without one, you get a nudge to keep your streak going, but only on days you have not practised yet.

For what the scores and streaks mean, see [How it works](/projects/brit-ready/docs/how-it-works).
