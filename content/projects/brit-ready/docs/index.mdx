Brit Ready is a local-first practice app for the Life in the UK Test. Rather than drilling question wording, it tracks every examinable fact on its own and keeps bringing back the ones you have not quite got, until a single readiness score says you are worth booking.

These pages explain what happens under the bonnet, so the numbers on your dashboard make sense.

## 🧭 What's here

- [🧠 How it works](/projects/brit-ready/docs/how-it-works) — the four study modes, how mastery and spaced revision are scored, and how the readiness score is built.
- [🔒 Your data](/projects/brit-ready/docs/your-data) — where progress is stored, backing it up and restoring it, reminders, and every setting.
- [🛠️ Development](/projects/brit-ready/docs/development) — running it locally, the content model, icons, CI and the one build detail that matters for deploying.

> Brit Ready is independent and non-official. Its questions are original and do not reproduce real exam questions.
