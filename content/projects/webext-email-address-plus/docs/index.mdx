title: 👋 Introduction
description: Email Address Plus fills email fields with a plus-addressed variant of your address, labelled with the site you are on.
---

Email Address Plus turns `alex@example.org` into `alex+shop.example.com@example.org` on the right site, at the right moment. Every sign-up gets its own address, so you can see which site handed yours on and filter its mail without trusting anyone.

Everything happens in the browser: the address is stored locally and the label is worked out from the page's hostname.
